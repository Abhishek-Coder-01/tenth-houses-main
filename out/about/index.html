02Our Story

Heritage, held with care

A house built on evenings, not inventory. Seventeen years of placing marquees where they belong.

Founded 2009

We do not rent tents.
We place evenings.

Tenth House began as a favour between two riggers, one in Copenhagen and one in Kyoto, who kept being asked to raise a tent for a wedding nobody else would take on. Seventeen years later we build a small, deliberate register of structures a year, and we still answer that same question first.

  • No catalogue.
  • No stock tents.
  • No poles where you did not ask for them.

A host is introduced to a structure the way a collector is introduced to a painting: with the full dossier, the drawings, and as much time as it takes.
